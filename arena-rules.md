# NutriLens Arena — Official Contest Rules

**Start:** Prizes are given for periods that start on Monday 9 November 2026 or later: first week 9–15 November 2026, first month December 2026, first half year January–June 2027. Duel prizes apply to duels that start on 9 November 2026 or later.

### 1. Organiser

The Arena contest is organised by the developer of the NutriLens app (contact: bahri9954@gmail.com). **Google LLC and Apple Inc. are not sponsors of this contest, do not endorse it and are not involved in it in any way.**

### 2. Entry

Entry is free. No purchase is necessary and a purchase does not improve the chance of winning. Every user with Arena points appears on the board. A user who does not want to appear can hide from the board in the Arena settings; hidden users cannot receive prizes.

### 3. Eligibility

Prizes go only to permanent accounts (signed in with Google or email). Guest accounts, accounts hidden from the board, suspended accounts and official accounts (Coach Nutri) cannot receive prizes. An ineligible person stays on the board and the prize goes to the next eligible person.

### 4. Periods

- Week: from Monday 00:00 UTC (03:00 Turkey time) to the next Monday.
- Month: from the 1st 00:00 UTC to the 1st of the next month.
- Six months: 1 January–30 June and 1 July–31 December.
- Duel: the length agreed by the two users.

### 5. Points

Points are counted per day (UTC) from these sources; a summary is shown in the app under the "?" icon in the Arena:

| Source | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Logging a meal | 10 each, at most 4 meals a day |
| Staying within ±10% of your daily calorie goal | 50 |
| Logging your weight | 20 |
| Reaching your water goal | 25 |
| Steps | 8,000 steps 30, 4,000 steps 15 |
| Sleep | 7–9 hours 25, 6–10 hours 12 |
| Logging a meal or your weight that day | 5 |
| Streak (days in a row with a meal logged) | 2 per day, at most 30 |
| **Daily cap** | **225** |

For prizes, only entries added at most 2 days after their own day count. If a goal is changed more than 2 days after a day, that day's calorie and water points do not count.

### 6. Prizes

| Period      | 1st             | 2nd             | 3rd             | Minimum active days |
| ----------- | --------------- | --------------- | --------------- | ------------------- |
| Week        | 15 days Premium | 10 days Premium | 5 days Premium  | 5                   |
| Month       | 30 days Premium | 15 days Premium | 10 days Premium | 20                  |
| Six months  | 45 days Premium | 30 days Premium | 20 days Premium | 120                 |
| Duel winner | 3 days Premium  |                 |                 | —                   |

For a duel prize: the duel lasts at least 7 days, both players use permanent accounts, a winner receives at most 2 duel prizes in one ISO week and the same two people win at most one duel prize together in one week. A tied duel has no prize.

### 7. Ties

On equal points, the person with more active days wins; next, the person who got there first (the earlier latest entry time among counted entries). There is no draw of lots.

### 8. Use of the prize

A prize is time-limited access to the Premium tier in the app. If a paid subscription is running, the prize days start when it ends; several prizes are added back to back. The prize has no cash value and cannot be transferred, sold or exchanged.

### 9. Fair play

Implausible manual entries and entries added later for an older day do not count for prizes. Cheating, automation, multiple accounts or any attempt to get around these rules leads to no prize or a withdrawn prize, and the account can be removed from the Arena.

### 10. Results

Results are determined within 72 hours after a period ends. The server first waits the 2 days allowed for late entries, then finishes duels and awards the period. The winner sees the prize screen when opening the app. Winners' shown Arena names (Tournament name; else profile name; else community name) are listed as recent winners. Each winner's name, profile photo and days won are posted in the community by Coach Nutri. People who hide in the Arena are never posted.

### 11. Personal data

The contest collects no new personal data. Arena name, points and prize records are processed under the NutriLens Privacy Policy (sections 1.6 and 1.15): https://orjinal63.github.io/nutrilens-legal/privacy.html . For GDPR and KVKK rights: bahri9954@gmail.com.

### 12. Changes and termination

Rule changes are announced in the app at least 7 days in advance and do not apply to a running period. The organiser may end the contest with the same notice; prizes already won are kept.

### 13. Contact and appeals

Questions and appeals go to bahri9954@gmail.com within 14 days after the results are published.
