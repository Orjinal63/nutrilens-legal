# NutriLens — Invite Program Rules

**Effective date:** November 21, 2026
**Last updated:** November 22, 2026 (rules 2–5: meal logs, account age, e-mail and monthly limit)
**Organiser:** the NutriLens developer (Bahri Bulut, FitnessAI)
**Contact:** bahri9954@gmail.com

The in-app "Invite friends" page shows the same rules.

---

1. **Unique link and code.** Every account has one unique invite link and code
   (8 characters, never 0, O, 1 or I). The link is the Google Play page with
   the code in the install referrer. The link and code belong to the account
   holder alone.
2. **When does a friend count?** When all five are done:
   1. they install NutriLens from the invite link, or enter the code within
      **7 days** of first opening the app;
   2. they finish the welcome questions;
   3. they create an account with Google, Apple or e-mail and confirm the e-mail;
   4. they log meals on **3 different days** (a day counts by the date the entry
      reaches our server; an entry added later for a past day is not a new day);
   5. their account is at least **7 days old**.

   A friend who has not met all of these yet stays "pending" and is checked
   again every night for 60 days after the invite.
3. **Not counted.** Inviting yourself; someone who already used another invite
   code or who invited you; guest accounts; an account whose e-mail address is
   the same as the inviter's or as one of the inviter's already counted
   friends'. Addresses are
   compared without upper/lower case and without the "+tag" part; for Gmail,
   dots are ignored too (first.last+1@gmail.com equals firstlast@gmail.com).
4. **Rewards (each step once):** 10 friends → 3 days, 25 → 7 days, 50 → 15
   days, 100 → 30 days of Premium, then every further 100 (200, 300, …) → 30
   days.
   **Monthly limit:** at most **30 friends** count for one inviter in any 30
   days. The rest stay pending and count as the limit frees up.
5. **How rewards are added.** The days are added automatically the moment a
   step is reached and start at once. While a paid subscription runs they wait
   and start when it ends (same rule as Arena prizes).
6. **Permanent account.** A permanent account is needed to receive rewards.
   Friends invited while a guest still count; the days are added on sign-up.
7. **No cash value.** Premium days cannot be transferred or sold.
8. **Fair use.** Fake accounts, bought installs and cheating do not count. If
   abuse is found, the days involved are taken back.
9. **Privacy.** An inviter sees counts only, never who the friends are. The
   invited friend does not see the inviter's details either.
10. **No sponsor.** Google and Apple are not sponsors and are not involved.
    Taking part is free; no purchase is needed.
11. **Changes.** Rule changes are announced in the app at least 7 days in
    advance. Days already earned are kept.
